# Global Health Eda

## Problem Statement
How do health spending, GDP, and life expectancy relate across countries, and which nations outperform their economic peers?

## Dataset
**Source:** WHO Global Health Observatory — health expenditure, life expectancy, and mortality indicators by country and year.

## Approach
- Cleaned, merged dataset from multiple WHO tables
- 8–10 publication-quality visualizations (matplotlib/seaborn)
- Statistical analysis of spending-to-outcome efficiency by region
- Narrative notebook with markdown explanations of each finding
